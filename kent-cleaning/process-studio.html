02 · TEACH

The practical-dad lesson format

Make it feel like someone trustworthy is standing beside you: show the outcome, explain why it matters, demonstrate the task and tell the truth about the mistake people make. A short vertical video is one delivery method. Every lesson should also work as a printable instruction card. This is a style cue, not a TikTok affiliation.

60-second field lesson

  1. Show the finished result.
  2. Name the task in one sentence.
  3. Show three visible actions.
  4. State the safety check.
  5. Show the common mistake.
  6. Show the proof and next step.

Coach check

  1. Can a beginner say the result back?
  2. Can they identify when to stop?
  3. Can they complete it under supervision?
  4. Can another person verify the proof?
  5. Can they explain which skill transfers?

04 · SPONSOR AND SCALE

Let companies sponsor a useful lesson

A sponsor can support equipment, training time, learner transportation or a community demonstration. Keep the process honest: identify the sponsor, state what they provided, and do not let payment turn a review into an unsupported claim.

Use “process pilot” or “licensed method” while the model is being tested. Do not sell it as a franchise until a franchise attorney reviews the actual name, control, assistance, required payments and state requirements. The FTC Franchise Rule requires extensive disclosure for covered franchise offers.
