Endless SI · Field Notes

Small notes on making a start

Begin with one useful question

A business idea becomes easier to test when you name one person, one problem, and one small result they could use. A clear question gives you somewhere to learn before you spend heavily or build too much.

A prototype is a question you can try

A screen demo can show a flow or reveal missing steps. It cannot prove that a physical product is safe, that a supplier can deliver it, or that customers will buy it. Label what has been tested and what still needs evidence.

Keep the first step small enough to finish

Choose a short task that leaves a visible artifact: one page, one conversation draft, one sample, or one worksheet. Review what you learned, then decide whether the next step is worth taking.

Start with one sentence

This first prototype demonstrates the workflow without claiming that a simulation is a certified physical product.
