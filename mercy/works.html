Faith expressed through care

Fourteen ways to show up.

The seven corporal works address bodily needs. The seven spiritual works care for the inner life. Together they inspire this network of practical help, presence, and prayer.

The corporal works

Food, shelter, care, and companionship in the realities of life.

01

Feed the hungry

Share a meal, provide groceries, or help a neighbor keep food on the table.

Explore this work of mercy →
02

Give drink to the thirsty

Help someone access clean water or keep water service connected.

Explore this work of mercy →
03

Clothe the naked

Offer suitable clothing, winter coats, work shoes, or laundry help.

Explore this work of mercy →
04

Shelter the homeless

Help a neighbor find or keep a safe place to live, including essentials for a home.

Explore this work of mercy →
05

Visit the sick

Offer a visit, a ride to an appointment, or practical support for a caregiver.

Explore this work of mercy →
06

Visit the imprisoned

Join an approved prison ministry, write through a permitted program, or support a family.

Explore this work of mercy →
07

Bury the dead

Help with burial needs and accompany families as they honor a loved one.

Explore this work of mercy →

The spiritual works

Compassion, understanding, forgiveness, and faith in everyday relationships.

01

Counsel the doubtful

Listen to questions of faith and help someone connect with a trusted, willing guide.

Explore this work of mercy →
02

Instruct the ignorant

Share knowledge of the faith with humility. Practical mentoring can also accompany this care.

Explore this work of mercy →
03

Admonish the sinner

Offer humble, loving correction in a relationship where guidance is welcomed; never publicly shame.

Explore this work of mercy →
04

Comfort the sorrowful

Be present with someone grieving or struggling. A willing listener can be a meaningful gift.

Explore this work of mercy →
05

Forgive injuries

Ask for help taking a step toward forgiveness, without pressure to restore unsafe contact.

Explore this work of mercy →
06

Bear wrongs patiently

Encourage patience and a peaceful response while respecting healthy boundaries.

Explore this work of mercy →
07

Pray for the living and the dead

Share an intention, pray with consent, or remember someone who has died.

Explore this work of mercy →
