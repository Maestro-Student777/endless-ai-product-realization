A practical scholarly workflow

From timeline to understanding

Find — select a person, year, saint, pope, council or source.
Read — retain the original wording and identify the source document.
Compare — distinguish chronology systems and conflicting historical claims.
Verify — consult primary texts, Church documents and credible scholarship.
Reflect — connect context with theology, virtue, liturgy and daily action.
Record — save the citation, uncertainty, interpretation and next question.
